    Permissions and errors

    What the panel shows when it needs you, when a step is refused, and when an engine fails.

    Replicate

    • A permission request appears under the step that asks, as .ap-ask: what it wants to do, the exact command or file, then Deny, Allow for this chat, Allow. The step group summary says "Waiting for you".
    • A denied step stays in the list, struck through, with "Denied" on the right.
    • Engine failures are an .ap-alert--error inside that engine's turn, with the CLI's reason in plain words and one action that fixes it.
    • No git repository: one .ap-alert--warn banner under the header. Accept and Reject are off until git exists.
    • Errors never use a modal or a toast; they stay in the thread where they happened.

    • Allow turns the step into a running step; Deny strikes it through.
